Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the puzzle game "Tsumu" (the "App") collects and how it is used. The App is a free game that runs in the Pi Browser.

Information we collect

  • Pi account information. When you sign in with Pi, the App receives your Pi username and user ID from the Pi platform. The App does not receive your passphrase or wallet keys.
  • Game data. Your hearts, coins, best score, and gifts sent to or received from other players are stored so the game can be resumed and shared between your devices.
  • On-device data. The App also stores game data in your browser's local storage as a backup.

How the data is stored

Game data is stored in Google Firebase (Firestore), a cloud database service provided by Google. Data is associated with your Pi user ID and username. We do not sell your information to third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

Heart gifts

If you send a heart to another player, the recipient can see the username of the sender. Please do not include personal information in your username expectations beyond what the Pi platform already shows.

Payments

The App does not process Pi payments and does not collect payment information. Hearts and coins in the App are virtual items with no monetary value.

Data deletion

You can ask for your stored game data to be deleted by opening an issue in the App's GitHub repository (github.com/tysseo7-meru). Deletion removes your game data from the database; data already delivered to another player's device (such as a received heart) cannot be recalled.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the App's features change. The current version will always be available at this URL.
